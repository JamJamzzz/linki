# Linki Agent Setup — Help Me Configure My AI Outreach Agent

I'm using **Linki**, a self-hosted LinkedIn and email outreach tool. It has a built-in AI agent that writes personalized cold emails and LinkedIn messages for every prospect in my campaigns.

The agent receives this data for each prospect before writing:
- Name, job title, company, location, seniority, tenure
- LinkedIn headline, About/bio, career history (up to 4 positions)
- Company: industry, size, description, tech stack
- The specific step instruction I wrote (e.g. "cold intro focusing on their tech stack")

I need to configure three things in the agent settings. Please ask me the questions below and then write the three fields for me.

---

## What I need you to write

### 1. About Your Company (`user_prompt`)

This is injected into every message the agent writes. It tells the agent who is sending the message, what the company does, who the ICP is, and how to write.

Keep it **under 200 words**. Be specific — the agent uses this to make the opening line relevant.

Include:
- Company name and what it does (one sentence)
- Who we sell to (ICP: industry, role, company size)
- Main differentiator or value proposition (one or two sentences, concrete)
- Writing style preferences (e.g. direct, no buzzwords, first-name basis, short sentences)
- Any topics or angles that tend to resonate with prospects
- Anything to avoid (e.g. "never mention pricing", "don't mention competitors")

### 2. Email Examples (`email_examples`)

2–3 real cold emails that worked well (good reply rate, or ones you're proud of). The agent matches the tone, structure, and opening style.

Format each as:
```
--- Example 1 ---
Subject: [subject line]

[email body]

--- Example 2 ---
...
```

If I don't have real examples yet, write 2 fictional but realistic ones based on my company and ICP that I can use as a starting point.

### 3. LinkedIn Message Examples (`linkedin_examples`)

2–3 real or example LinkedIn connection messages or DMs. Same idea — agent matches tone and length.

Format:
```
--- Example 1 ---
[message body]

--- Example 2 ---
...
```

LinkedIn messages should be shorter than emails: 2–3 sentences max, no subject line, no greeting needed.

---

## Questions to ask me

Please ask me these questions (you can ask them all at once):

1. What is your company called and what does it do in one sentence?
2. Who do you sell to? (role, industry, company size — be specific)
3. What is the main problem you solve, and what makes you different from alternatives?
4. What tone do you prefer? (e.g. casual/direct, warm/professional, formal)
5. What language do your prospects speak? (e.g. English, German, mixed)
6. Do you have any real emails or LinkedIn messages that got good replies? (paste them — or I'll write examples)
7. Anything the agent should never say or do?

Once I answer, write all three fields ready to paste.
